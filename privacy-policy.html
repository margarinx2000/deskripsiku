Privacy Policy — DeskripsiKu

Last updated: 26 November 2026

DeskripsiKu is an Anna App that generates product descriptions in Bahasa Indonesia for online sellers. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: DeskripsiKu has no server of its own, collects no personal data, and never receives your information outside the Anna platform.

What the app processes

When you use DeskripsiKu, you type in product details: a product name, category, key features or materials, an optional price, a target marketplace (Tokopedia, Shopee, TikTok Shop, or WhatsApp), and a writing tone. Those details — and only those details — are sent to Anna's built-in AI models through the platform's sampling feature so the descriptions can be generated. The generated descriptions, selling points, and hashtags are returned to you in the app window.

What the app stores

The app saves your most recent form inputs and the most recent generated result in Anna's per-app storage, tied to your own Anna account, so the window reopens where you left it. This data stays inside the Anna platform, is visible only to you, and is removed when you uninstall the app. The developer cannot see it.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, payment details, or marketplace account credentials.
  • It does not connect to Tokopedia, Shopee, TikTok Shop, WhatsApp, or any other third-party service.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • It requires no API keys and makes no network calls outside Anna.

Platform processing

Description generation is performed by Anna's AI models under the Anna platform's own terms and privacy policy, which govern how the platform processes the text you submit. DeskripsiKu's developer receives none of that data.

Children

DeskripsiKu is a business tool intended for sellers; it is not directed at children.

Changes
